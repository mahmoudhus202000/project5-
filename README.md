Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of flight delays across major U.S. airlines and airports. The dataset includes over 1.9 million flights, focusing on delay causes, carrier performance, and temporal patterns.

The report is divided into multiple interactive pages:

Overview Page: Presents total flight numbers, delay distribution, and key metrics.

Average Delay by Carrier: Compares airlines based on average delay minutes per cause (carrier, weather, aircraft, etc.).

Sum Delay by Carrier: Highlights total delay minutes per airline and categorizes them by cause.

Delay by Airport: Identifies airports with the highest total delays.

Time Analysis: Examines how flight delays vary by month across the year.

The dashboard enables data-driven insights for airline performance improvement, operational efficiency, and better resource planning.

Key Insights
✈️ Overall Flight & Delay Summary

Total Flights: 1.937 million

Total Delayed Flights: 1.937 million — indicating that every recorded flight had some delay minutes (likely a dataset of delayed flights only).

Total Delay Time: 60.2 million minutes

🧭 Delay Causes Breakdown

Carrier Delays: 24M minutes (≈30%) — the largest contributor to total delays.

NAS (National Airspace System) Delays: 18.7M minutes (≈24%) — operational and traffic-related causes.

Crew Delays: 31.6M minutes — a significant operational issue.

Weather Delays: 4.6M minutes — relatively minor impact.

Security Delays: 112K minutes — minimal influence.

Insight: Operational and crew-related delays dominate the dataset, showing the need for better scheduling and crew management.

🧳 Airline Performance

Southwest Airlines and American Airlines have the highest total delay times, exceeding 8–10 million minutes combined.

Mesa Airlines and Atlantic Southeast Airlines record significantly lower total delay times, suggesting better punctuality or fewer flights.

On average, AirTran Airways and JetBlue Airways experience the longest average delays (20–25 minutes per delayed flight).

Insight: Major carriers handle more flights but face higher delay totals; smaller regional carriers perform better in punctuality.

🛫 Airport Delay Insights

Top 3 airports with the highest total delays:

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL) – 4M minutes

Chicago O’Hare International Airport (ORD) – 3M minutes

Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) – 2M minutes

Insight: Busy hub airports experience the most severe delays due to high traffic volume and congestion.
